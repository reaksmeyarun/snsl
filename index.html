01 Your library +

All your moments, at a glance.

Browse your photos and videos in one smooth, date-grouped library, with a floating glass tab bar and quick Select mode for bulk actions.

  • Fast loading
  • Select mode
  • Phone and tablet support
02 Collections +

Everything, neatly organised.

Find albums, photos, videos, screenshots, selfies, portraits, recordings, favorites, hidden items, recently deleted files, downloads and QR codes.

  • Media categories
  • Hidden and favorites
  • Quick access
03 Albums and folders +

Your folders, your way.

Open albums with full-bleed covers, browse real device folders and manage files across internal storage and SD cards.

  • Folder browsing
  • Move and rename
  • Copy files
04 Photo and video preview +

Stay in the moment.

View photos and videos with quick controls to play, scrub, favorite, inspect details, edit, share or delete.

  • Playback controls
  • Quick actions
  • Media details
05 Search +

Find that moment faster.

Search your library, narrow results by date and filter by media type to get to the right memory.

  • Date filters
  • Media type filters
06 Photo editor +

Make every photo yours.

Crop, rotate, flip and adjust your photos. Start with Auto tools including Enhance, Dynamic and AI Enhance.

  • Crop and rotate
  • Adjustments
  • AI Enhance
